Operational Reality

Stress doesn't always announce itself.

Uniformed personnel operating in high-stakes environments experience cumulative pressures that build silently over time.

01

Extended Deployment

Prolonged separation from home environments and continuous high-alert operations.

02

Long Duty Hours

Sustained high-tempo shifts demanding constant cognitive and physical vigilance.

03

Consecutive Shifts

Repeated rotation cycles without sufficient downtime for deep physiological recovery.

Intelligence Framework

One signal is rarely enough.

AURA integrates multi-signal indicators to provide a comprehensive, holistic assessment of welfare risk.

1. Duty Signals

  • Duty duration & consecutive days
  • Night-duty frequency

2. HR Signals

  • Leave utilization & deficits
  • Deployment duration

3. Voluntary Wellness

  • Self-reported stress check-ins
  • Sleep quality & fatigue scores
