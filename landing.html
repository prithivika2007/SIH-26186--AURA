The Problem

Warning signs are missed until it's too late

Uniformed personnel face relentless duty hours, night shifts, and high-stress operations — yet welfare checks remain manual, infrequent, and reactive.

Reactive, not proactive

Checks happen after burnout is visible, not before.

No unified signal

Duty rosters, leave patterns, and self-reports live in silos.

Stretched welfare staff

Too few officers to manually track every individual's wellbeing.

How AURA Helps
