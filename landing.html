The Core Challenge

Warning signs are missed until it's too late

Uniformed personnel face relentless duty hours and night shifts — yet current welfare mechanisms remain manual and reactive.

Reactive, Not Proactive

Interventions typically happen after burnout or fatigue becomes visible on duty, rather than preventing it beforehand.

Fragmented Signals

Duty rosters, leave utilization records, and personal wellbeing check-ins live in complete organizational silos.

Stretched Welfare Staff

A very limited number of welfare officers are expected to manually monitor and support hundreds of active personnel.

Unique Capabilities

Engineered for Operational Excellence

Explainable AI Risk Tiers

No black-box scores. Every flag outlines exact reasons like consecutive night shifts and missed check-ins.

DutyFair AI Balancer

Dynamically rebalances patrol and duty loads across team members to prevent localized burnout hot spots.

AURA Live Voice Companion

Natural voice interaction enabling personnel to express stress and receive immediate coping guidance.

Resilience Forecasting

Predictive 7-day unit readiness trends that help commanders adjust rest windows ahead of operations.

Built on Trust, Not Surveillance

AURA is architected so personnel never fear being monitored. Individual health insights are strictly restricted to medical and welfare officers, while commanders view aggregated, anonymized trends.

Command Anonymization

Commanders see unit health status and readiness indices, never individual names or scores.

Strict Access Control

Personnel-level risk flags are accessible exclusively by authorized welfare officers for support.

Consent-Driven Design

Full transparency on what telemetry is gathered, ensuring complete confidence across ranks.
